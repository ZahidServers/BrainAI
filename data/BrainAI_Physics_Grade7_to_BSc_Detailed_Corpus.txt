SOURCE BASIS
This corpus is an expanded teaching corpus built directly from the Physics progression supplied by the user in this conversation. It follows the listed Grade 7–12 NCERT progression and the listed B.Sc. Physics core architecture. It is not a reproduction of any textbook.

DEPTH POLICY
Every listed microtopic below contains a concept statement, five concrete examples, diagnostic checks, and modelling cautions. The examples are deliberately varied: conceptual, numerical, application-based, comparison-based, and limiting-case reasoning where the topic permits it.

IMPORTANT
• Formulae are presented with conventional notation; exact sign conventions must be stated when solving numerical optics/mechanics problems.
• School-level sections prioritize conceptual clarity before mathematical formalism.
• B.Sc. sections assume calculus, linear algebra, differential equations and mathematical maturity.
• This is a training corpus, not a substitute for a university's exact examination syllabus.
• Where a topic is inherently qualitative, the five examples are physical/application examples rather than forced numerical exercises.

Grade 7 — Heat and Temperature — MICROTOPIC 1
TOPIC: HEAT VS TEMPERATURE
CORE IDEA: Heat is energy transferred because of a temperature difference; temperature indicates thermal state and is related to average microscopic kinetic energy.

FIVE DETAILED EXAMPLES:

Example 2: A hot cup transfers energy to cooler surroundings until thermal equilibrium is approached.
Example 3: Two objects at 30 °C have the same temperature but need not contain the same thermal energy.
Example 4: A large bucket and small cup of water can have the same temperature but different total internal energy.
