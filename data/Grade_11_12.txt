EDUCATIONAL AI MASTER CORPUS — ORIGINAL TEACHING TEXT
============================================================

SCOPE
Kindergarten Junior + Senior, Grades 1–12, NCERT + Maharashtra
Balbharati aligned, including Science, Commerce, Arts/Humanities and
Maharashtra bifocal/vocational domains.

This corpus is ORIGINAL educational writing. It is curriculum-aligned
rather than a reproduction of NCERT or Balbharati textbooks. It is intended
as a foundation for an educational AI and can be expanded with more chapters,
worked examples, question banks, laboratory activities, projects and
multilingual variants.

DATA DESIGN
Each chapter contains:
- learning objectives
- teaching text
- key terms
- examples/applications
- practice questions
- answer guidance
- misconception check
- revision guidance

TRAINING RECOMMENDATION
For supervised fine-tuning, convert each chapter into multiple records:
1) concept explanation
2) question-answer
3) worked problem
4) misconception correction
5) summarisation
6) age-level simplification
7) exam-style response
8) application scenario
9) comparison
10) retrieval fact
