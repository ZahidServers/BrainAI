list and terminology rather than silently replacing it with a different taxonomy.

TOPIC      : Empathize
DIFFICULTY : Foundational
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. DEFINITION
Empathize is the research-oriented stage of design thinking in which designers seek to understand users, their context, goals, constraints and lived experience before defining solutions.

2. DEEP THEORY / EXPLANATION
Empathy work uses observation, interviews and contextual inquiry to understand what people actually do rather than relying only on assumptions about what they say they do. Findings become evidence for later problem framing.

3. CORE CONCEPTS
1. context of use
2. user goals
3. pain points
4. behaviors
5. motivations
6. constraints

4. KEY EQUATIONS / RELATIONSHIPS

5. APPLICATIONS

4. problem discovery

6. COMPARISONS
