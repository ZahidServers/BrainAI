BIOLOGY MASTER CORPUS — DETAILED TRAINING TEXT
====================================================

PURPOSE
-------
A broad biology knowledge corpus spanning school biology (Grades 7–12),
undergraduate/postgraduate biology and zoology, professional biomedical
curricula, specialized biology, and human/animal/plant disease concepts.

STRUCTURE
---------
Each entry contains:
1. Definition
2. Core concepts
3. Five worked examples
4. Five question prompts
5. Optional notes

This is an educational corpus. Clinical entries are educational descriptions,
not individualized medical advice. Medical knowledge, diagnostic criteria and
treatment recommendations can change and should be checked against current
authoritative clinical guidance.

SOURCE COVERAGE
---------------
The corpus was expanded from the user's supplied biology curriculum, including:
school biology, B.Sc./M.Sc. biology, MBBS, BDS, BUMS, BHMS, B.Sc./M.Sc.
zoology, diseases of humans/animals/plants, biophysics, omics, chronobiology,
ethology, synthetic biology and phylogenetics.

CHAPTER : Grade 7
TOPIC   : Nutrition in Plants
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DEFINITION
Nutrition in plants is the acquisition and use of materials needed for growth, maintenance and reproduction. Autotrophic plants manufacture organic food, mainly through photosynthesis.

CORE CONCEPTS
1. Autotrophic nutrition uses light energy, carbon dioxide and water to form carbohydrates.
2. Chlorophyll absorbs mainly red and blue wavelengths and participates in light capture.
3. Stomata permit gas exchange and also influence water loss by transpiration.
4. Parasitic plants obtain nutrients from living hosts; Cuscuta is a common example.
5. Saprotrophs obtain nutrients from dead organic matter, while lichens represent a symbiotic association of a fungus with a photosynthetic partner.

FIVE EXAMPLES

Example 2: Chlorophyll absorbs mainly red and blue wavelengths and participates in light capture.
